# Code of conduct

MeshArc's repositories are places to get work done together. Be someone others are glad to work with.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be specific and constructive in reviews and issues.
- Welcome people new to the project, and answer the question that was asked.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or comments about anyone's identity, appearance or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or using issues to advertise.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break this code. To report a problem, write to [hello@mesharc.dev](mailto:hello@mesharc.dev); reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
